# Independent semantic review — Chinese introduction and sections A–C

Reviewed the complete `guide-abc.zh.md` against the English introduction and Steps 01–31 of `STEP_BY_STEP.md`. This is an independent assessment of translation meaning and executable instructions, not a new validation of the model's constitutive assumptions or a live COMSOL Desktop click-through. The translator's existing `guide-abc-review.md` already records structural, code, link and numeric-token checks; those were not mechanically repeated here.

No substantive translation error or missing scientific qualification was found. No correction is required before integration.

The following high-consequence passages were checked directly:

- Introduction, lines 5–7: post-activation time zero, absence of later forcing, closed adiabatic ideal-gas source, uniform pressure, finite available work, constant carrier density and outlet inertance, pre-existing outlet, approximate fixed meniscus entry pressure, intact undrained incompressible gel, stress-free reference wall, zero initial velocities, ambient far-field/exit pressure, and the positive outward/expansion sign convention are preserved. Rebound, phase exchange, rupture and target impact remain excluded.
- Steps 08–09, lines 89–147: `p0` and `pInf` remain absolute pressures; `E0` remains a derived reversible work expression. The available work is explicitly mechanical availability after activation, not incident laser energy. The finite liquid inventory and effective inertia length remain distinct. Changing `tauLaser` alone still cannot change the initialized-state solution.
- Steps 10–12, lines 149–213: fixed A chamber radius and zero wall speed remain explicit. Actual discharged energy, axial impulse and mass remain separate from effective column energy/momentum. The outgoing `-rho*U^2/2` term and `Eout` contribution to conservation remain required. The Chinese phrase “排出流体的动压项” is appropriate to the pressure-form Bernoulli expression. Confinement force remains one hemisphere's axial resultant; dynamic pressure remains an outlet scale, not impact pressure. Pressure communication remains a diagnostic without a solved propagation field.
- Steps 13–20, lines 215–314: both equation quantities remain dimensionless; all five A state initial values remain zero; `ut_0=0` is distinguished from zero physical acceleration; derivative naming remains distinct from multiplication. Study and solver time lists must match. BDF Strict, initialization Off and the four active Negative (<0) stopping rules retain their exact meanings. Inventory, declared 20% stretch and Mach screens remain separate; the stretch screen is explicitly not a measured rupture threshold. Communication is checked after solving rather than treated as an online stop row.
- Steps 23–27, lines 334–438: absolute chamber pressure, source overpressure and outlet dynamic pressure are correctly distinguished. Stored column energy and actual emitted energy are not interchanged. Temporal maxima are separate from spatial maxima and need not occur simultaneously. Last refers to the actual last stored row of the stopped solution, not 100 µs or the exact zero-crossing root.
- Steps 28–31, lines 440–488: acceptance still requires conservation, positive gas volume/absolute pressure, outward motion, finite inventory, communication and Mach screens. Reproducing the controls is distinguished from PFC/laser calibration. Source work is selected through the absolute-pressure inversion while `E0` remains derived. The nine-case maximum remains conditional on the declared family, and energy-only bounds remain distinct from attained speed/dynamic pressure. Excluded activation and target impact receive no pressure prediction.

COMSOL menu paths, parameter/state names, field settings, import/reference distinctions, result dataset selection and All/Maximum versus Last/None instructions retain their operational meaning. English control names paired with brief Chinese labels serve Desktop navigation and do not create paragraph-by-paragraph bilingual prose.

Notation and derivation conventions

符号与推导约定

SI units are used. Absolute pressure belongs in phase equilibrium and equations of state. Mechanical pressure differences state their reference. Each chapter defines its own normal orientation before interface equations; a liquid-to-vapor normal and a solid-to-liquid normal serve different interfaces.

使用国际单位制。相平衡及状态方程使用绝对压力。力学压差明确其参考压力。各章在界面方程之前定义法向；液体指向蒸气的法向与固体指向液体的法向用于不同界面。

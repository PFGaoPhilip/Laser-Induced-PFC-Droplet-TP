Primary references and scope

一手文献与引用范围

  1. [R1] Rayleigh (1917). On the pressure developed in a liquid during the collapse of a spherical cavity. DOI: 10.1080/14786440808635681.

    [R1] Rayleigh (1917)。 球形空腔塌缩时液体中产生的压力。DOI：10.1080/14786440808635681。

    Classical ideal spherical collapse; the singular endpoint is an applicability limit.

    经典理想球形塌缩；奇异终点表示适用范围的边界。

  2. [R2] Cooker & Peregrine (1995). Pressure-impulse theory for liquid impact problems. DOI: 10.1017/S0022112095003053.

    [R2] Cooker & Peregrine (1995)。 液体冲击问题的压力冲量理论。DOI：10.1017/S0022112095003053。

    Pressure impulse and frozen-geometry short-event momentum balance.

    压力冲量及冻结几何的短时动量平衡。

  3. [R3] Supponen et al. (2016). Scaling laws for jets of single cavitation bubbles. DOI: 10.1017/jfm.2016.463.

    [R3] Supponen et al. (2016)。 单个空化气泡射流的标度规律。DOI：10.1017/jfm.2016.463。

    Boundary/asymmetry effects on internal collapse jets; not an exterior PFC-jet calibration.

    边界及非对称性对内部塌缩射流的影响；不是外部 PFC 射流的标定。

  4. [R4] Peters et al. (2013). Highly focused supersonic microjets: numerical simulations. DOI: 10.1017/jfm.2013.26.

    [R4] Peters et al. (2013)。 高度聚焦的超声速微射流：数值模拟。DOI：10.1017/jfm.2013.26。

    Spatial meniscus focusing under stated pressure and geometric boundaries.

    指定压力及几何边界下的空间弯液面聚焦。

  5. [R5] Liang et al. (2022). Comprehensive analysis of spherical bubble oscillations and shock wave emission in laser-induced cavitation. DOI: 10.1017/jfm.2022.202.

    [R5] Liang et al. (2022)。 激光诱导空化中球形气泡运动及激波发射的综合分析。DOI：10.1017/jfm.2022.202。

    Single-event laser-cavity energy partition, thermal state and compressibility; no harmonic-acoustics prerequisite.

    单次激光腔体事件的能量分配、热状态及可压缩性；不设简谐声学先修路线。

  6. [R6] Strohm et al. (2011). Vaporization of perfluorocarbon droplets using optical irradiation. DOI: 10.1364/BOE.2.001432.

    [R6] Strohm et al. (2011)。 光照引发全氟碳液滴汽化。DOI：10.1364/BOE.2.001432。

    Formulation-specific optical PFC activation; not a universal transfer velocity or threshold.

    特定配方的 PFC 光学激活；不是普适转印速度或阈值。

  7. [R7] Wei et al. (2014). Laser-induced cavitation in nanoemulsion with gold nanospheres for blood clot disruption: in vitro results. DOI: 10.1364/OL.39.002599.

    [R7] Wei et al. (2014)。 金纳米球纳米乳液中激光诱导空化用于血栓破碎：体外结果。DOI：10.1364/OL.39.002599。

    Absorber-containing PFH activation with aggregation/geometry qualifications.

    含吸收体 PFH 的激活，需考虑团聚及几何限制。

  8. [R8] NIST Chemistry WebBook. Pentane, dodecafluoro- (CAS 678-26-2). DOI: 10.18434/T4D303.

    [R8] NIST Chemistry WebBook。 十二氟戊烷（CAS 678-26-2）。DOI：10.18434/T4D303。

    PFP molar mass, measured phase data and range-limited Antoine correlation; heat-capacity entry uses its listed temperature.

    PFP 摩尔质量、实测相变数据及有限温度区间的 Antoine 关联式；热容采用所列测量温度。

    Separate liquid heat-capacity entry: the reported measurement temperature and molar-to-mass conversion are retained in Chapter 2.

    单独的液相热容条目：第二章保留所报告的测量温度及摩尔量到质量量的换算。

  9. [R9] NIST Chemistry WebBook. Hexane, tetradecafluoro- (CAS 355-42-0). DOI: 10.18434/T4D303.

    [R9] NIST Chemistry WebBook。 十四氟己烷（CAS 355-42-0）。DOI：10.18434/T4D303。

    PFH named reference data; formulation and temperature dependence remain explicit.

    PFH 具名参考数据；明确保留配方及温度依赖性。

  10. [R10] Feng et al. (2007). Competing fracture in kinetically controlled transfer printing. DOI: 10.1021/la701555n.

    [R10] Feng et al. (2007)。 动力学控制转印中的竞争断裂。DOI：10.1021/la701555n。

    [R11] Li et al. (2024)。 水凝胶复合印章实现激光驱动非接触气泡转印。DOI：10.1073/pnas.2318739121。

    Water-vapor-cavity/composite-stamp deformation and release. This does not establish embedded-PFC coherent jet stabilization.

    水蒸气腔体／复合印章形变及释放；不能据此确立嵌入 PFC 的相干射流稳定作用。

  12. [R12] Brujan et al. (2001). Dynamics of laser-induced cavitation bubbles near an elastic boundary. DOI: 10.1017/S0022112000003347.

    [R12] Brujan et al. (2001)。 弹性边界附近激光诱导空化气泡的动力学。DOI：10.1017/S0022112000003347。

    Deformable-boundary interaction and jet directions depend on stiffness and geometry.

    可变形边界相互作用及射流方向取决于刚度和几何。

  13. [R13] Gaudron, Warnez & Johnsen (2015). Bubble dynamics in a viscoelastic medium with nonlinear elasticity. DOI: 10.1017/jfm.2015.7.

    [R13] Gaudron, Warnez & Johnsen (2015)。 具有非线性弹性的黏弹性介质中的气泡动力学。DOI：10.1017/jfm.2015.7。

    Finite-strain intact-medium elastic resistance; damage and layered pocket mechanics need other closures.

    有限应变完整介质的弹性阻力；损伤及分层液体口袋力学需其他闭合关系。

  14. [R14] Eggers & Dupont (1994). Drop formation in a one-dimensional approximation of the Navier–Stokes equation. DOI: 10.1017/S0022112094000480.

    [R14] Eggers & Dupont (1994)。 Navier–Stokes 方程一维近似下的液滴形成。DOI：10.1017/S0022112094000480。

    Slender free-surface transport and pinch-off model; not a spatial jet-launch solution.

    细长自由表面输运与夹断模型；不是空间射流发射解。

  15. [R15] NIST Chemistry WebBook. Water (CAS 7732-18-5). DOI: 10.18434/T4D303.

    [R15] NIST Chemistry WebBook。 水（CAS 7732-18-5）。DOI：10.18434/T4D303。

    水相变数据；293 K 与 323 K 使用各自区间的 Antoine 系数。

Algebraic, dimensional, conservation and numerical controls check the declared models. They do not calibrate a target formulation or establish experimental transfer performance. Every claimed new material benefit remains conditional until its optical, phase, flow, fracture and reset links are measured or resolved in the appropriate model.

Laser Induced PFC Droplet TP

激光诱导 PFC 液滴转印

Follow a laser pulse through phase change, jet formation, and transfer. Derive each balance and check its limits.

沿激光脉冲、相变、射流形成到转印展开学习。推导各项平衡并核验其限制。

Laser absorption → heating and nucleation → finite phase inventory → bubble and spatial jet → transmitted load → fracture and placement.

激光吸收 → 加热与成核 → 有限相存量 → 气泡与空间射流 → 传递载荷 → 断裂与定位。

Five chapters and Appendix A contain worked derivations and examples. Each unit ends with three defense questions. Chapter 3 includes the resolver study. Chapter 5 chains theory, calculation and solver verification for a jet on a rigid wall. Appendix A includes both modeling studies.

五章与附录 A 提供完整推导及例题。每单元设三个答辩问题。第三章包括求解器研究。第五章依次给出射流撞击刚性壁面的理论、计算与求解器核验。附录 A 包括两项建模研究。

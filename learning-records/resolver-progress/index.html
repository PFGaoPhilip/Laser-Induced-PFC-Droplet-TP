1. Physical problem and prerequisites

1. 物理问题与准备

Model activated PFC in water near a rigid wall. Compute phase conversion, heat transfer, compressible flow and interface motion. The domain is axisymmetric. Basilisk x maps to axial z; y maps to radial r. Positive z points away from the wall at z=0. Positive r points away from the axis. Initial velocity is zero and PFC is liquid. Initial pressure is 101325 Pa. Temperature and inventory are specified inputs. This pilot uses closed slip far boundaries. Nonreflecting production boundaries remain unqualified. Read Sections 2–7 before compiling with Basilisk C and qcc.

模拟刚性壁面附近水中的受激 PFC。计算相变、传热、可压缩流动及界面运动。采用轴对称域。Basilisk 的 x 对应轴向 z，y 对应径向 r。正 z 离开 z=0 的壁面。正 r 离开对称轴。初始速度为零，PFC 为液体。初始压力为 101325 Pa。温度与物质总量为给定输入。该试算采用封闭滑移远边界。非反射生产边界尚未合格。使用 Basilisk C 和 qcc 编译前，阅读第 2–7 节。

2. State variables and geometry

2. 状态变量与几何

M_PFC and M_water store partial mass densities in kg/m³. E_PFC and E_water store total energy densities in J/m³. Shared momentum density q has units kg/(m²·s). Z_PFC stores PFC vapour mass density in kg/m³. f_PFC and a_water are volume fractions. Recover specific internal energy before calling the EOS. Physical integrals require the axisymmetric metric. Basilisk dv() includes radial weighting. Multiply by 2π once. Adjust radius to match the fixed inventory on the mesh.

M_PFC、M_water 保存部分质量密度，单位 kg/m³。E_PFC、E_water 保存总能量密度，单位 J/m³。共享动量密度 q 的单位为 kg/(m²·s)。Z_PFC 保存 PFC 蒸汽质量密度，单位 kg/m³。f_PFC、a_water 是体积分数。调用状态方程前先恢复比内能。物理积分需要轴对称度量。Basilisk 的 dv() 已含径向权重。2π 乘一次。调整半径以匹配网格上的固定物质总量。

3. Thermodynamics and conservative evolution

3. 热力学与守恒演化

Use the bundled material bridge and thermodynamic coefficients. One potential determines water pressure, entropy and temperature. PFC inversion enforces common pressure, mixture volume and mixture energy. Retain branch selection and admissibility checks. Constant sound speed, Tait water or ideal gas would change this closure. The balances below describe the continuum model. Native split operators supply the discrete closure. Material exchange sources must cancel. Check geometry and energy closure together.

使用随附材料桥接代码和热力学系数。同一势函数确定水的压力、熵和温度。PFC 反演满足共同压力、混合体积和混合能量约束。保留分支选择与可接受性检查。常声速、Tait 水或理想气体会改变该闭合。下式描述连续模型。原生分裂算子提供离散闭合。材料交换源必须抵消。几何与能量闭合共同检查。

4. 算子分裂与回滚

Save all fields, geometry and diagnostic counters before each trial. Choose the smallest remaining-time, maximum-step and acoustic CFL limit. Apply half steps for phase, interface heat, water conduction and viscosity. Reconstruct faces and advect conserved fields. Apply pressure and capillarity, then reverse the half-step sequence. Rebuild geometry after each modifying operator. Advance time and iteration after success. On refusal, restore every saved quantity and check restoration. Candidate018–030 interventions remain documented numerical assumptions. Retain soft-accepted heat diagnostics. Second-order convergence is not established. Missing: refinement evidence.

# Rights and provenance

Delivery is to the owner for research reference. Newly authored Stage3 scripts, prose, tables and report sources are identified by the final file manifest. No additional third-party front-end assets are necessary unless explicitly listed with their license in this package.

Copied T/E/A and full_closure documents are retained evidence belonging to the existing research workflow. They are not represented as newly validated scientific results. Historical paper citations are reference links; publisher PDFs and inspected page images are excluded. This package grants no new rights to cited papers or imported evidence.

The candidate030 run snapshot includes existing solver/code resources and an executable, supplied as hash-bound evidence. Basilisk-derived resources retain their upstream licensing obligations (see source headers and bundled [GPL3 notice](licenses/Basilisk_GPL3.txt)); the existing [CoolProp MIT notice](licenses/CoolProp_MIT.txt) is also retained. Their presence is not a complete distribution of the upstream build system. No full solver rebuild or public redistribution claim is made. Original code with third-party notices must retain those notices.

The manifest records byte hashes and original relative source paths. Rebuilding the documentation needs only bundled data and existing Python/TeX tooling, not external source PDFs or a running solver.

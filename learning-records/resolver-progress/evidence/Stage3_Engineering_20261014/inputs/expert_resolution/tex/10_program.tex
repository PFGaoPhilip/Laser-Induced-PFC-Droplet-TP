\section{Minimal experimental program}\label{sec:program}
Each measurement targets a named gap. Together with the missing compressible/receiver/gel models
and a feasible-design optimization, they can turn Benchmark statement~\ref{prop:B1} into a
calibrated device envelope. Measurements alone do not prove a global optimum.
Items are ordered by their leverage on the answer.
\begin{center}\small
\begin{longtable}{p{0.03\textwidth}p{0.29\textwidth}p{0.28\textwidth}p{0.27\textwidth}}
\toprule \# & Measurement & Closes & Why necessary\\\midrule\endhead
1 & Single-droplet bubble radius history $R(t)$ through expansion, collapse and rebound in water
($\ge5$\,Mfps, 3--12\,$\mu$m PFP and PFH) & condensation coefficient $\alpha_{\mathrm c}$ (fit $R_{\min}/R_{\max}$ and
rebound to Section~\ref{sec:spherical}) & collapse violence, jet speeds and collapse pressures change by up to two
orders of magnitude across $\alpha_{\mathrm c}$.\\
2 & Absorbed energy per droplet and activation probability versus fluence; absorber-free and droplet-free
controls & activation route (homogeneous $T_{\mathrm k}$ versus heterogeneous), $\Phi_{\mathrm a}$ & sets the rigorous
budget and the source-pressure ceiling; heterogeneous onset lowers both.\\
3 & Maximum radius $R_{\max}$ versus absorbed energy & tests $R\le R^*$ and the real-EOS expansion & a measured
$R_{\max}>R^*$ for the same measured inventory/state challenges the full energy-reference,
EOS and H0--H4 assumptions (e.g.\ additional heating, prestress or breakdown).\\
4 & Jet imaging at controlled stand-off $\gamma=1.25$--4 near a glass receiver & directed-jet predictions of
Table~\ref{tab:bimpfc} & validates the spherical-content closure and the PFC growth shape.\\
5 & Hydrophone or fibre-optic probe at a known distance with stated area and bandwidth & O5/O7 & the only way to
compare a pressure prediction with data; requires the sensor response (Limits E12--E13).\\
6 & High-rate gel modulus, viscosity and fracture energy (inertial cavitation rheometry, Abeid et al.\ 2024; needle
or laser cavitation fracture tests) & B2 arrest, rupture gate, $A_{\mathrm c}\le\Phi_{\mathrm a}/G_{\mathrm c}$ & decides whether a
fluid path and an exterior jet can exist.\\
7 & Dissolved gas content, droplet coating and size distribution & content cushion, nucleation sites & the cushion
and heterogeneous nucleation enter items 1--2.\\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\end{center}

\textbf{Excluded or failed states.} BIM runs that rebounded without impact (frozen at $\gamma\ge3$; KM
$\alpha_{\mathrm c}=0.1$ PFP at $\gamma=4$) are reported as ``no impact'' and not used for maxima. Gel cases that arrested
two-phase are reported as such. No run was discarded silently; all are in \texttt{data/}.
The three requested PFH $N=96$, cap-0.02 growth aborts and targeted $N=128$, cap-0.01
pre-impact abort are retained in the refinement records. Their raw speeds are excluded;
the finer $96\to128$ endpoint remains unconfirmed.

\textbf{Unresolved (cannot be bounded or predicted from the present evidence).}
(U1) The peak spherical collapse pressure after the Mach event (6\,$\mu$m water samples: PFP
$\alpha_{\mathrm c}\ge0.5$, PFH already $0.3$): requires a compressible multiphase solver with a
valid PFC vapour EOS at supercritical states. (U2) Fast-condensation jets beyond the working screen,
direct contact on the receiver ($\gamma\lesssim1$) and near-contact needle jets: require viscous
compressible simulations (Lechner et al.\ 2020). (U3) Gel-embedded directed jets and
exterior transfer through a fracture: require rate-dependent fracture data and a coupled fluid path. (U4) The
stress-confined source: outside the PFP EOS. (U5) The actual device: absorbed state, nucleation route,
$\alpha_{\mathrm c}$, geometry and sensor unknown (Section~\ref{sec:program}). The actual PFC maximum for the user's
device is therefore \textbf{unresolved}. The conditional thermodynamic ceilings and sampled reduced-model
envelope are resolved as stated; the continuous benchmark optimum and fast-condensation physical
maximum are not established.

\section{Second-pass audit}\label{sec:audit2}
Symbols and units were checked locally and against the notation table; equation identifiers X\# are unique,
and prior-document equations retain their document names. Reported thermodynamic/sweep/envelope values
are regenerated by \texttt{scripts/make\_tables.py}; fixed controls, literature values and verification
figures retain explicit source/record scope. The local dimension, branch and limiting-case checks
are supplemented by the verification files in Appendix~\ref{app:verif}.
The cavity-radius ceiling is approached by the
inviscid dynamics to within $\PFPRsharpDyn$\% (PFP) and $\PFHRsharpDyn$\% (PFH); jet energies are below
$\Phi_{\mathrm a}$ in the reported profiles; the constant-pressure benchmark is within 6\% of published speeds.
Resolved sampled collapse speeds increase with condensation until the Mach event censors them;
event equality does not establish convergence of a physical peak. Source inspection levels
are declared individually (Section~\ref{sec:refs}), including textbook premises not re-inspected.
This completion audit includes collaborating agents' internal checks and independent published
benchmarks; no independent external expert review or experimental PFC-device validation is claimed.

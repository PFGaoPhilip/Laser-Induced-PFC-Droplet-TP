\textbf{Energy--phase--mass ledger for the benchmark (6\,$\mu$m PFP).} Absorbed heat $Q=\PFPQSix$\,nJ (to activate
$\mF=\PFPmSix$\,ng at $T_{\mathrm k}$) $\to$ exergy $\Phi_{\mathrm a}=\PFPPhiSix$\,nJ (the remaining
$Q-\Phi_{\mathrm a}$ is unavailable heat at the outset) $\to$ during expansion: content work $\PFPledgerW$\,nJ, of which
$\PFPledgerpV$\,nJ displaces the ambient, $\PFPledgerSurf$\,nJ is surface energy, $\PFPledgerVisc$\,nJ viscous loss,
$\PFPledgerRad$\,nJ acoustic radiation, while $\PFPledgerContent$\,nJ of exergy remains in the cold vapour
$\to$ during collapse: the jet takes $\PFPenvEfmin$--$\PFPenvEfmax$\% of $\Phi_{\mathrm a}$ depending on the closure
(Table~\ref{tab:bimpfc}). Mass: the whole PFC inventory vaporises (flash fraction $>1$) and recondenses to a degree
set by $\alpha_{\mathrm c}$. No efficiency coefficient is assumed at any stage.

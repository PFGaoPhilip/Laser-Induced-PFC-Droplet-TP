# Mission: Laser Induced PFC Droplet TP
# 使命：激光诱导 PFC 液滴转印

## Why
## 学习目的

Explain and calculate the complete physical chain needed to explore laser-activated PFC droplet transfer, compare liquid/PVC/hydrogel architectures, and design discriminating experiments.

解释并计算探索激光激活 PFC 液滴转印所需的完整物理链，对比液体／PVC／水凝胶结构，并设计能够区分机制的实验。

## Success looks like
## 成功标准

Explain the causal mechanism, quantitative balance, assumptions and failure conditions of each of the five chapters through three defenses in your own words.

通过每章三个用自己语言作答的答辩，解释五章的因果机制、定量平衡、假设与失效条件。

Carry a finite PFC inventory into a physically consistent array/jet output and assess whether its actual load path supplies selective fracture, intact landing and reset. Report the highest verified single-shot output separately from repeatable intact transfer.

从有限 PFC 存量出发得到符合物理约束的阵列／射流输出，并评估实际受力路径能否实现选择性断裂、完整落地及复位。将已核验的最高单次输出与可重复完整转印分开报告。

## Constraints
## 约束

One connected project route; bilingual paragraphs; fully worked necessary derivations, local symbols and variable diagrams; formula-first reference answers; no mastery without demonstrated understanding.

采用单一连贯项目路线；双语段落；完整展开必要推导、公式前符号定义及变量图；参考答案先引用原公式；未经理解展示不得判定掌握。

## Scope
## 范围

Theory and declared analytical teaching controls. Future target calibration, spatial device solutions and experiments remain explicit next research steps. Software-operation lessons and previous COMSOL outputs are excluded from this theory course.

理论及声明条件的解析教学对照。目标标定、特定装置空间解及实验明确列为后续研究工作。软件操作课程及此前 COMSOL 输出不纳入本理论课程。

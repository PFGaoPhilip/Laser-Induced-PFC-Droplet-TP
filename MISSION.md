# Mission: Laser Induced PFC Droplet TP
# 使命：激光诱导 PFC 液滴转印

## Why / 目的

Explain laser-driven PFC droplet transfer.
Calculate the balances along its physical chain.
Compare liquid, PVC, and hydrogel architectures.
Design experiments that distinguish their mechanisms.

解释激光驱动 PFC 液滴转印。
计算其物理链中的各项平衡。
比较液体、PVC 与水凝胶结构。
设计能够区分其机制的实验。

## Success / 成功标准

Explain each unit’s mechanism, assumptions, and failure conditions.
Demonstrate understanding through three defense questions per unit.
Connect finite PFC inventory to jet output and transmitted loads.
Assess selective fracture, intact landing, and reset.
Report single-shot output separately from repeatable transfer.

解释各单元的机制、假设与失效条件。
通过每单元三个答辩问题展示理解。
贯通有限 PFC 存量、射流输出与传递载荷。
评估选择性断裂、完整落地与复位。
分别报告单次输出与可重复转印。

## Constraints / 约束

Use paired English and Chinese.
Retain complete derivations, local definitions, and variable diagrams.
Reference answers begin with formulas.
Keep language and day/night controls available.

采用配对英中文本。
保留完整推导、公式前定义与变量示意图。
参考答案从公式开始。
提供语言与日夜切换控件。

## Scope / 范围

Five chapters and Appendix A provide theory and worked controls.
Chapter 3 includes the resolver implementation study.
Appendix A includes finite-energy and embedded-PFC modeling studies.
Each model retains its assumptions and validation limits.
Target calibration, spatial device solutions, and experiments remain research work.

五章与附录 A 提供理论及已解对照。
第三章包括求解器实现研究。
附录 A 包括有限能量与嵌入式 PFC 建模研究。
各模型保留其假设与验证限制。
目标标定、装置空间解与实验仍属于研究工作。
